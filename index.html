ISOTOPE

Infrastructure for ethical, unstoppable,
self-learning exchange of data and AI.

Three Pillars

Data

Query-response without disclosure. Banks, insurers, hospitals exchange answers — not data.

AI

Distributed inference. Lightweight models on nodes. Simple queries answered nearby. Complex go to data center.

People

The messenger as entry point. Communication without censorship, blocking, or surveillance.

How It Works

One pattern: query-response without disclosure.

«Has this client defaulted?» → «No».
«What is the risk coefficient?» → «0.88».
«Is this certificate valid?» → «No».
«Translate this text» → The nearest AI node answers.

Ready-Made Scenarios

Certificate Verification

3 nodes needed

Check any certificate in 2 seconds. No documents. No waiting.

Credit Check Without Disclosure

3–5 nodes needed

Ask «Has this client defaulted?» Get «No» — not the full credit history.

Simple AI Queries

5–10 nodes needed

Translation, summarization, classification — answered by the nearest node.

Anti-Fraud

5–10 nodes needed

Block fraudulent transactions before they happen. Answer in 1–2 seconds.
