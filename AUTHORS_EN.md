## How the Project Was Created

ISOTOPE was created by a human. All key decisions — architecture, ethical model, the design of the immune system, 
the choice of technologies — were made by the author of the project. The human determines what is built and why, 
verifies the result, and is responsible for it.

In implementation, the author was assisted by tools — in the same sense in which developers are assisted by IDEs, 
compilers, libraries, and code generators. Among them were AI assistants: they performed individual technical tasks 
under the author's guidance — wrote fragments of code in Go and Flutter, helped with refactoring and documentation drafts. 
Like any tool, they accelerate the work but do not replace the author: the concept, the ethical core, 
and the responsibility remain with the human.

Copyright on the code belongs to the Keeper.
