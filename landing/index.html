What Is ISOTOPE

ISOTOPE is an architecture for data exchange where the source is never disclosed and the answer is verified by the network. Not a messenger. A protocol.

Query-Response Without Disclosure

Data never leaves its owner. Only the answer matters. «Default?» → «No». «Risk?» → «0.88». «Valid?» → «Yes».

Ethical Immunity

Every message is compared to the ethical hash. Harmful content fades. Valuable content stays. No censorship — immune system.

Immunity Activates With Scale

2–3 nodes: secure channel. 15+ nodes: immune system. 100+ nodes: unstoppable network.

Four Layers, Two Vectors

Technical protocol. Philosophical ethics. Business trust market. Application messenger. Enterprise + Community.
