# ISOTOPE Use Cases

How ISOTOPE solves real problems you face every day.

## 1. Banking and Finance

### Credit Scoring Without Disclosing Full History

**The pain today:**
You lose a client because getting a loan requires them to disclose
their full credit history, which they don't want to show even to the bank.

**How it's solved now:**
The bank contacts a credit bureau and buys a full report.
The client feels exposed and goes to another bank that asks for less data.

**How ISOTOPE solves it:**
The bank sends a query to the ISOTOPE network:
«Has this client defaulted in the last 2 years?»
The answer is simply «Yes» or «No». No history. No diagnosis.

### Anti-Fraud: Catching Fraudsters Before They Disappear

**The pain today:**
You see a fraudster moving money while you're waiting
for a response from another bank.

**How it's solved now:**
You call that bank, explain the situation, wait for verification.
Two days later you get a response — but the money is already gone.
The fraudster outran you.

**How ISOTOPE solves it:**
You send an anonymous query to the network:
«Is this account linked to fraud?»
The answer arrives in 1-2 seconds.
You block the transfer before it happens.

### Cross-Border Payments Without SWIFT

**The pain today:**
You pay huge commissions for international transfers through SWIFT.

**How it's solved now:**
You delay payments by several days to minimize the number of transactions.
Your suppliers wait, and you lose reputation.

**How ISOTOPE solves it:**
Your bank connects to ISOTOPE as a node.
The payment goes directly through the network in real time.
Commission is minimal. No intermediaries.

### Verifying Counterparty Authority

**The pain today:**
Your employees spend weeks coordinating contracts with counterparties,
verifying their authority.

**How it's solved now:**
Lawyers and accountants send official requests, wait for responses,
manually double-check documents and make phone calls.
Errors cost time and money.

**How ISOTOPE solves it:**
You query ISOTOPE: «Is the signatory's authority valid?»
The answer arrives instantly.
The contract is concluded in 5 minutes, not 5 days.

## 2. Insurance Companies

### Risk Assessment Without Disclosing Diagnoses

**The pain today:**
A client refuses insurance when they learn they must disclose
all their medical diagnoses.

**How it's solved now:**
You try to convince them, offer a discount.
They still go to a competitor because they don't want
third parties knowing about their health.

**How ISOTOPE solves it:**
You query: «Cardiovascular risk coefficient?»
The answer is simply a number, for example «0.88» (low risk).
The client doesn't disclose diagnoses, but you get accurate data
for rate calculation.

### Anti-Fraud: Detecting Multiple Claims

**The pain today:**
You pay insurance to the same fraudster who took out policies
with three different companies.

**How it's solved now:**
You suspect the client is dishonest, but you can't query data
from other insurers — it violates confidentiality.
The fraudster thrives, and you bear the losses.

**How ISOTOPE solves it:**
You send a query to the ISOTOPE network:
«Have there been payouts for this case at other companies?»
Answer: «Yes, 2 payouts in the last year.»
You deny the claim and block the fraudster.

### Medical Data Exchange Without Violating Confidentiality

**The pain today:**
Doctors don't want to transfer your clients' data, even for risk assessment,
fearing violation of medical confidentiality.

**How it's solved now:**
You ask the client to sign voluntary consent for data transfer,
but this extends application processing by weeks and irritates the client.

**How ISOTOPE solves it:**
The client connects to ISOTOPE.
The hospital (or its node) directly answers a query you cannot read —
you receive only a digital response (risk coefficient),
without diagnoses or personal information.

## 3. Medicine and Healthcare

### Research on Rare Diseases

**The pain today:**
You want to find a cure for a rare disease, but there are only 300 patients
worldwide, and you cannot access their data due to medical confidentiality.

**How it's solved now:**
You spend years manually collecting data through rare international
conferences and closed chats. You lose time, and patients lose hope.

**How ISOTOPE solves it:**
Your ISOTOPE query: «What is the average glucose level in patients
with symptom X over the last year?»
300 hospitals provide an anonymous aggregated response.
You get statistics instantly, without violating anyone's confidentiality.

### Anonymous Patient Pool

**The pain today:**
As a patient with a rare disease, you feel isolated.

**How it's solved now:**
You spend years searching for other patients with the same diagnosis
through forums and social networks, risking your privacy.

**How ISOTOPE solves it:**
Your ISOTOPE node confirms the presence of a symptom (anonymously).
The network creates an «anonymous pool» for scientists.
You don't reveal yourself, but you help science.

### Doctor-to-Doctor Consultation

**The pain today:**
A doctor wants to consult another specialist about your complex case,
but cannot send them your data without your consent.

**How it's solved now:**
The doctor loses time trying to rephrase the query or discuss cases abstractly.
The consultation drags on for days.

**How ISOTOPE solves it:**
The doctor sends a narrow query to the network:
«Do you have cases with symptoms Y and reaction to drug Z?»
A colleague answers: «There were 3 such cases» —
without revealing the patient's identity or even knowing where the query came from.

## 5. International Arbitration

### Cross-Border Award Recognition

**The pain today:**
Your company won a court case in Germany, but you cannot get the award
recognized in Brazil because the judicial system is slow and politically dependent.

**How it's solved now:**
You hire Brazilian lawyers, go through all instances, wait years,
and spend hundreds of thousands of dollars on attorneys.
The outcome is not guaranteed.

**How ISOTOPE solves it:**
The parties initially agree to arbitration in ISOTOPE.
The award is recorded in the network.
It is automatically recognized by any node, wherever it is located.
A smart contract executes the award without your involvement.

### Arbitrator Reputation

**The pain today:**
You don't know if the arbitrator you entrusted with resolving your dispute is honest.

**How it's solved now:**
You choose an arbitrator by recommendation, but cannot verify their reputation.
You risk corruption or incompetence.

**How ISOTOPE solves it:**
An ISOTOPE arbitrator is a certified node with high weight.
Their reputation (number of successful resolutions) is visible in the network.
Their decision can be challenged, but it is automatically verified by other nodes.
Corruption is impossible.

## 10. Society and Government

### Internet Censorship

**The pain today:**
You live in a country where the government blocks websites and messengers
during political events.

**How it's solved now:**
You switch to VPN or Tor, which the government also tries to block.
Speed drops, reliability drops, you feel isolated.

**How ISOTOPE solves it:**
ISOTOPE has no central server.
Blocking individual IP addresses or protocols does not disable the network,
because it masks traffic as regular HTTPS.

### Charity Transparency

**The pain today:**
You want to help earthquake victims, but you don't know
if the money will reach them.

**How it's solved now:**
You transfer money to a charitable foundation, but you cannot verify
how much will reach the goal (often less than 30% arrives).

**How ISOTOPE solves it:**
You send tokens directly to the ISOTOPE network.
The recipient is a verified node.
Audit trail in the network: you see when and to whom tokens were transferred.
Recipient anonymity is preserved, but you see that the help arrived.
